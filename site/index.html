Methods: Two complementary analyses were performed: an ecological study of district‑level HCPS notifications (2005‑2022) using a negative‑binomial mixed‑effects model with demographic and environmental predictors, and an individual‑level logistic mixed‑effects analysis of hospitalized patients (2010‑2020) evaluating age, sex, indigenous affiliation, hospitalization duration and surgical interventions.
