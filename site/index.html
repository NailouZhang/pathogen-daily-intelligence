A Stochastic Hantavirus Model with Environmental Reservoir

Background: Hantaviruses circulate among rodents, contaminated environments, and humans, causing severe syndromes with no licensed antiviral. Methods: A coupled SEIR‑SEIV stochastic model links a rodent reservoir, environmental viral pool, and dead‑end human SEIR population. Results: The model is well‑posed, yields an additive R0 separating direct and indirect transmission, proves R0 as the exact threshold with globally stable equilibria, remains globally positive under Brownian noise, identifies shortcomings of the classic Ito‑corrected threshold, introduces a variational exponent Λ (<0 forces extinction), and reproduces observed seasonal and interannual HFRS peaks (periodic R≈2.15 vs R0 = 1.63).
