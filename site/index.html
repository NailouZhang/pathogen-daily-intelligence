Fractional optimal control of hantavirus transmission between rodents and humans

Background: This paper studies a fractional optimal control problem for a hantavirus transmission model involving rodent reservoirs and humans. Methods: A modified SIR–SIR model with Caputo fractional derivatives and three time‑dependent controls is analyzed using Pontryagin’s principle, and solved numerically via a fractional Adams–Bashforth–Moulton predictor‑corrector with forward‑backward sweep. Results: Combined control yields the greatest reduction in infected humans and rodents, and memory‑dependent dynamics modify transmission trajectories and optimal intervention profiles. Contribution: The study offers a theoretical framework for applying fractional‑order optimal control to hantavirus management, highlighting memory effects. Limitations: The model lacks empirical calibration and validation with surveillance data, and only abstract‑level information is provided.
