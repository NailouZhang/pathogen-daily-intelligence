arenavirus et hantavirus chez les petits mammiferes sauvages terrestres de madagascar identifies par rt-pcr

Background: Arenaviruses and hantaviruses cause severe human disease and are rodent/insectivore‑associated, but had not been detected in Madagascar (F1‑F3). Methods: A prospective study sampled 586 small wild mammals in Danorana forest (Oct 2008‑Mar 2009), testing liver and spleen by RT‑PCR and sequencing (F5‑F8, F10‑F11). Results: No arenavirus was found (0 %); hantavirus RNA was present in 35.9 % of animals, revealing two putative new species linked to Rattus rattus and Oryzorictes hova (F9‑F15). Contribution: First documentation of hantavirus circulation in Madagascar and identification of two novel hantavirus candidates (F13). Limitations: Novel lineages need full‑genome confirmation; sampling was limited to one region, tissues, and time frame, and detailed analyses are not reported (F16).
