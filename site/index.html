Hantavirus mutagenesis is impacted by mutagens and host antiviral proteins

Background: Hantaviruses are emerging zoonotic viruses with high human fatality, and the mechanisms of their mutagenesis are poorly understood. Methods: Mutational spectra were calculated and compared across 14 divergent hantavirus species. Background: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used for health information, but their reliability for hantavirus risk communication during the 2026 Andes virus outbreak is uncertain.
