Background: HCPS, caused by Andes virus, is a severe zoonotic disease with a significant public‑health burden in Chile. Methods: Two complementary analyses were performed: an ecological negative‑binomial mixed‑effects model of district notifications (2005‑2022) using demographic and fire‑related predictors, and a logistic mixed‑effects model of hospitalized patients (2010‑2020) assessing age, sex, indigenous status, stay length and surgeries.
