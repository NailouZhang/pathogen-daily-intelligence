Methods: Researchers reviewed Argentine ANDV outbreaks, extracted contact tracing and genetic data, calculated serial intervals, and estimated Rt and dispersion (k) using a Bayesian hierarchical model; they simulated outbreak scenarios with a branching‑process model. Results: Pooled Rt was 0.74 (95% CrI 0.28–1.29) and k 0.64 (95% CrI 0.36–1.16); 23.4% of cases caused 80% of transmissions; mean serial interval 21.92 days (SD 6.98); simulations predicted 95% of trajectories would stay below 15 cases in 10 weeks, with sustained transmission probabilities of 20.9% historically and 28.6% for the cruise‑ship scenario.
