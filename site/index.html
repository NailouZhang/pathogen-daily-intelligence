Ecological and Individual-Level Factors Associated With Hantavirus Cardiopulmonary Syndrome Notifications and in-Hospital Mortality Among Hospitalized Patients in Chile, 2005-2022.

Background: HCPS caused by Andes virus is a severe zoonosis with a major public‑health impact in Chile, and gaps exist in understanding ecological and individual mortality drivers. Methods: Researchers conducted an ecological negative binomial mixed‑effects analysis (2005‑2022) of district notifications using demographic and fire‑related variables, and a logistic mixed‑effects analysis (2010‑2020) of hospitalized patients with clinical and demographic predictors.
