CD8+ regulatory T cells limit immunopathology but impair viral clearance in Hantaan virus infection.

Background: HFRS caused by Hantaan virus involves CD8+ T cells that mediate viral clearance and immunopathology. Methods: Researchers characterized an HLA‑E (Qa‑1)‑restricted CD8+ regulatory T‑cell subset in HFRS patients and a murine HTNV model. Results: CD8+ Tregs (CD122+Eomes+CD49dloKIR+) were reduced in patients, correlated with severity, and showed a terminal phenotype; two Qa‑1‑binding HTNV peptides were identified; immunized wild‑type mice had less renal pathology, whereas D227K knock‑in mice cleared virus better but had worsened pathology; WT CD8+ Tregs suppressed effector proliferation via peptide‑specific, TGF‑β‑dependent mechanisms, while the D227K mutation altered inhibitory receptor expression and reduced antigen‑specific suppression. Contribution: The work shows HLA‑E/Qa‑1‑restricted CD8+ Tregs limit immunopathology at the expense of delayed viral clearance, offering a potential therapeutic axis for HFRS. Limitations: Only abstract information is available; detailed methodology, sample sizes, and statistical data are not reported.
