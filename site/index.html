Transmission timing and containment risk in human Andes virus outbreaks

Background: The 2026 multinational Andes virus outbreak linked to cruise‑ship travel from Argentina triggered extensive contact tracing and quarantine. Contribution: The findings support rapid, comprehensive contact tracing and quarantine after large international exposure events despite substantial costs. Limitations: Onward transmission risk could not be precisely estimated from early cases, and only an abstract is available.
