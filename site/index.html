Background: Mammalian hantaviruses cause severe disease, but most entry mechanisms are unknown. Methods: Researchers used rVSVs with diverse hantavirus Gn/Gc proteins to infect primary human endothelial cells, including PCDH1‑knockout cells, and performed binding and neutralization assays. Development of a rapid RPA-CRISPR/Cas12b assay for dual detection of Hantaan virus and Seoul virus.

Background: Hantaan virus and Seoul virus cause hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome, a global public health challenge. Methods: The study developed a rapid, high‑sensitivity assay combining recombinase polymerase amplification with CRISPR/Cas12b fluorescence detection, using virus‑specific sgRNAs and RPA primers targeting conserved genomic regions, performed in a single‑tube reaction for each virus. Results: Analytical sensitivity reached 1×10¹ copies/test in three technical replicates; no cross‑reactivity with a four‑organism non‑target panel; and all nine RT‑qPCR‑positive rodent samples were detected, showing complete positive agreement. Contribution: The assay demonstrates potential as a screening tool for dual detection of HTNV and SEOV, supporting further development. Limitations: The detection threshold is preliminary and does not account for extraction losses or matrix inhibition; negative agreement, specificity, false‑positive rate, and overall accuracy could not be assessed due to lack of negative samples; larger studies with well‑characterized specimens are needed.
