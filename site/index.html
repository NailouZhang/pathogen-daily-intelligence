Background: Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome caused by hantavirus remains a significant global public‑health burden. Methods: A panel of sera from convalescent patients and vaccinated individuals was tested for neutralizing antibodies against vaccine and epidemic hantavirus strains using an ELISA‑based microneutralization assay.
