Background: Andes orthohantavirus causes hantavirus cardiopulmonary syndrome with high mortality and no licensed vaccine. Methods: Immunoinformatics predicted CTL, HTL, and B‑cell epitopes from the glycoprotein precursor, filtered them for antigenicity, allergenicity, toxicity, and subcellular location, linked them with β‑defensin adjuvant, modeled the construct, docked to TLR‑3, performed normal mode analysis, immune simulation, and in‑silico codon optimization (CAI 0.97, GC 49.77%). Limitations: Findings are based only on in‑silico data from an abstract; experimental validation is required.
