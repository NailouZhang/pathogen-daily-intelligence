Background: The 2026 multinational Andes virus outbreak linked to cruise‑ship travel from Argentina raised concerns about onward transmission and the need for large‑scale contact tracing. Methods: Researchers used transmission‑pair data from the 2018‑2019 Epuyen outbreak and a mechanistic model of viral growth, symptom onset, and transmission, then simulated outbreaks with calibrated (R=2.12, k=0.316) and pooled (R=0.24, k=0.39) parameters.
