Network-Guided Drug Repurposing for Hantavirus Infection: Pathway Analysis, Target Identification, and Molecular What-If Simulations

Background: Hantaviruses cause severe disease in >200,000 people annually and lack approved antivirals. Methods: A three‑stage computational pipeline used transcriptomics of infected endothelial cells, a STRING PPI network to identify 15 hubs, Open Targets to find six druggable hubs and 12 candidates, and an ODE IFN‑signalling model testing eight drug perturbations. Results: Early IFN‑β cleared virus fastest (3.5 days) with lower CXCL10; early JAK inhibition delayed clearance (4.9 days) and raised CXCL10; IFN‑β plus anti‑CXCL10 antibody gave optimal antiviral and immunopathology balance. Contribution: Offers a mechanistic, evidence‑ranked framework to prioritize therapies and phase‑specific combos for preclinical hantavirus studies. Limitations: Only abstract data; no experimental validation reported.
