Segment-Aware Entangled Quantum Classifier for Imbalanced Hantavirus Host Prediction

Background: Host classification from viral genomes is difficult due to high dimensionality and class imbalance. Methods: Sequences are encoded with DNABERT‑2, compressed by a bottleneck autoencoder, and classified with the SEQC quantum model; training uses a balanced subset and testing on the full imbalanced set in a safe simulation. Anthropogenic drivers of recent ebola and hantavirus outbreaks: A one health perspective.

Background: Recent Ebola and hantavirus outbreaks have highlighted anthropogenic environmental change as a key driver of zoonotic emergence. Main directions: Deforestation, agricultural expansion, land‑use change and ecosystem degradation disrupt wildlife‑human interfaces, fostering spillover. Current state: Evidence links these drivers to altered wildlife ecology, viral evolution and increased host‑pathogen contact for both viruses. Gaps: Predictive models show expanding Ebola risk under current trajectories, revealing uncertainty about future hotspots and limited outbreak‑specific data. Future research: Calls for preventive strategies targeting environmental determinants via integrated One Health actions across health, agriculture and environmental sectors.
