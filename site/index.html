Background: Hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome (HFRS) is a zoonotic disease linked to orthohantaviruses in rodent hosts. Structural basis for protocadherin-1 recognition by New World hantaviruses

Background: Hantaviruses are rodent‑borne pathogens causing severe disease with no approved therapeutics; New World hantaviruses like Andes virus use protocadherin‑1 (PCDH1) for entry. Methods: Near‑atomic resolution structural determination of PCDH1 bound to the ANDV spike, mutagenesis of Gn loops, and analysis of four neutralizing antibodies were performed. Results: PCDH1 engages an asymmetric deep pocket formed by four Gn protomers; three Gn loops shape the binding site and allow transfer of PCDH1 binding to Old‑World glycoproteins; antibodies block binding by occluding the site or sterically hindering receptor access. Contribution: These findings define the structural basis of hantavirus‑receptor recognition, explain viral masking of the binding site, and guide antiviral therapeutic design. Limitations: Only an abstract is provided; detailed methods, quantitative results, and in‑vivo validation are not reported.
