Differences in Ecological Drivers for Leptospira and Hantavirus in an Urban Norway Rat Population.

Background: Rodent control is vital because urban rats carry zoonotic pathogens; evidence‑based programs must integrate rodent and pathogen ecology; this work sought ecological drivers of Leptospira and Hantavirus in Norway rats.
