Molecular surveillance of Bartonella species, Coxiella burnetii, and orthohantaviruses in wild small mammals and their associated chigger mites in South Korea.

Background: Wild small mammals are important hosts of diverse zoonotic pathogens, underscoring surveillance needs. Methods: From 2022–2025, 1,444 mammals and 12,869 chigger mites (680 pools) from 17 South Korean sites were PCR/RT‑PCR screened for Bartonella, Coxiella burnetii, and orthohantaviruses with sequencing. Results: Apodemus agrarius made up 84.9% of captures; B. schoenbuchensis DNA was in 69.6% of mite pools, other Bartonella spp. in 0.2% of livers; C. burnetii in 3.5% of livers (autumn 2024); orthohantavirus in 2.6% of mammals (94.6% A. agrarius) and 0.4% of mite pools.
